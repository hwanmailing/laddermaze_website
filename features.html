Explore. Think. Survive.

Discover what makes Ladder Maze
a unique and thrilling adventure!

Find the Safe Path

Each floor has two destinations:
the Key and the Exit.
Follow the clues, avoid hidden spikes,
and find the safe route.

  • Look for the Key
  • Find the Exit
  • Avoid hidden traps

Discover the Crystal Floors

Crystal Floors are safe and reveal important clues.
They appear where the Key Path and Exit Path overlap, showing part of a shared route.

  • Safe to stand on
  • Reveal the route
  • A key to the puzzle

Read the Numbers

Numbers carved into nearby Floors indicate
how many surrounding Floors contain hidden spikes.
Use the clues to avoid danger and find the safe path.

  • Numbers = hidden spikes
  • Like Minesweeper
  • More clues, better decisions

Master Your Weapons

Each enemy has different weapons, ranges, and behaviors.
Enemies attack automatically when you’re in sight.
Positioning, timing and strategy are key.

  • Auto attack (line of sight)
  • Different weapons & ranges
  • Fight, avoid, or wait

Use the Ladders

Ladders let you observe and analyze the maze
without stepping on dangerous Floors.

  • Check multiple levels
  • Watch enemy movements
  • Find Crystal Floors and plan your route

Think Before You Move

One wrong step can end your run.
Analyze the situation, check the clues,
and plan your next move carefully.

  • Is it safe?
  • Where is the enemy?
  • Does it lead to the Key or Exit?
