Privacy Policy

Website, game saves, and ads — explained simply.

Here is how the Arcade Game Lab website and Ladder Maze
handle information, and what stays on your device.

1Website

You can browse this website without an account or entering personal details. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics tools, or tracking cookies to these pages.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in its hosting documentation.

The footer’s email link simply opens your email app; it does not submit a website form. If you choose to send a message, your email address and anything you include are received so we can reply.

2Game Records

Ladder Maze saves scores, highest floors reached, and record dates on your device to display your best results. These records are stored locally, rather than sent to an Arcade Game Lab score server. No player account is required.

Local records remain until the app’s stored data is removed. Clearing app data or deleting the app can remove them. Device backup and restore settings may also affect whether those records are retained.

3In-App Purchases

If you make an in-app purchase, Apple or Google handles payment. The game checks purchase status and saves whether purchased features are unlocked. Arcade Game Lab does not receive your payment card details.

4In-Game Ads · AdMob

Ladder Maze uses Google AdMob to show in-game ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash or performance information. An IP address can also be used to estimate a general location.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, support advertising analytics, and help prevent fraud. The information used and whether ads are personalized depend on the platform, permissions, and applicable privacy settings.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and the AdMob data disclosures for Android and iOS.

5Ad Privacy Settings

Your device’s privacy settings provide controls for advertising identifiers or app tracking, depending on the operating system. Limiting personalization does not necessarily stop ads or the processing needed for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Google explains its advertising controls in Advertising — Privacy & Terms.

6Policy Updates

This page describes the website and game’s current information handling. If those features change, this explanation and the date below will be updated.
